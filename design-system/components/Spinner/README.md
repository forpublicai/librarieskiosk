# Spinner

A small ring that turns while the kiosk works, with an optional word beside it.

## Props
- `label`: what is happening ("Making your image…", "Thinking…"). Use it almost always.
- `size`: pixels, default 22.

## Do and do not
- Do pair the spinner with words. Do not show a spinner alone for more than a second.
- For jobs over 10 seconds (video, music) show progress too.
